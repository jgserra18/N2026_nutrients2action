Position papers & session materials

From Nutrient Metrics to Action

Linking budgets and efficiency across scales to policy and consumer choices

N2026, Kyoto N2026 conference website Tuesday 3 November, Room C-2 (1st floor)

A shared question

This session brings together perspectives on nitrogen budgets, efficiency, footprints, policy, and consumer choices. The position papers set out the contributors’ arguments and questions to help focus the discussion.
