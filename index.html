01

From nitrogen metrics to decisions: building the implementation architecture for action

Morten Graversgaard · Department of Agroecology, Aarhus University

Key claim · Nitrogen metrics become actionable when the right information reaches the right actor at a real decision point through trusted decision-support, advisory, and governance arrangements.

PDF

Questions for discussion

  1. Should integration start from the metrics, or from the decisions we want to influence: who needs to act, what can they change, and which nitrogen information do they need?
  2. How can implementation outcomes feed back into metrics, models, and policy so that nitrogen decision support becomes an adaptive learning system?
